Support

Help for Self-Discipline Check on Apple Watch.

App not opening or updating?

Make sure your Apple Watch is paired with your iPhone and has a working internet connection. If needed, restart the Watch and try installing or updating the app again.

Where is my data stored?

Your streak, daily check-ins, FAP records, and counters are stored locally on your Apple Watch. Self-Discipline Check does not use an external account or server for these records.

Need help or want to report a problem?

You can open a support issue on GitHub. Please do not include passwords, account credentials, private health details, or other sensitive personal information.

技术支持

Self-Discipline Check Apple Watch 版帮助页面。

应用无法打开或更新？

请确认 Apple Watch 已与 iPhone 正常配对并连接网络。必要时可重启 Apple Watch 后重新尝试安装或更新。

数据保存在哪里？

连续天数、每日打卡、FAP 记录和相关计数保存在 Apple Watch 本地。本应用不使用外部账号或服务器保存这些记录。

反馈问题

可通过上方 GitHub 支持入口提交问题。请勿在公开反馈中填写密码、账号凭据、私人健康信息或其他敏感个人信息。
