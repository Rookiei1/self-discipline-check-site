Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Short version: Self-Discipline Check does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. App records are stored locally on your Apple Watch.

Data collection

Self-Discipline Check does not collect personal information, health information, identifiers, usage analytics, advertising data, location data, or tracking data.

Local data

Streaks, daily check-ins, FAP records, and related counters are stored locally on the user's Apple Watch using on-device storage. The app does not send this data to an external server.

Accounts, analytics and advertising

The app does not require an account and does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or profiling services.

Data sharing

Because the app does not collect your data, it does not sell or share personal data with third parties.

Medical disclaimer

Self-Discipline Check is a general self-discipline and habit-tracking tool. It is not a medical service and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change in a future version, this page will be updated before those changes are released where required.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, please use the support page.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 10 日

简要说明：Self-Discipline Check 不收集、上传、出售或共享你的个人数据。应用记录保存在你的 Apple Watch 本地。

数据收集

本应用不收集个人信息、健康信息、设备标识符、使用分析数据、广告数据、位置数据或追踪数据。

本地数据

连续天数、每日打卡、FAP 记录及相关计数仅保存在用户 Apple Watch 的本地存储中，不会上传至外部服务器。

账号、分析与广告

本应用无需注册账号，不使用第三方广告、分析、追踪或用户画像服务。

医疗声明

Self-Discipline Check 是通用的自律与习惯记录工具，不属于医疗服务，也不提供诊断、治疗或专业医疗建议。
